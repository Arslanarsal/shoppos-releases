# ShopPOS owner guide

## Starting

Double click **ShopPOS** on the desktop. The app checks for a new version, then opens your shop. Without internet it simply opens. Everything works offline.

## Every day

**Make sale** (the blue button)
1. Type the item name or number. Pick it from the list.
2. Check the quantity and price. Add a discount if you give one.
3. Press **Finish sale**. Check the total. Press **Confirm**.
4. Press **Print** for the receipt.

**Record purchase** when you buy stock. Pick the items, type how many and the price you paid for each, then **Save purchase**. If the item is new, press **+ New item**.

**Add item**
1. Press **+ Add photo** and choose a picture. The app keeps its own copy, so you can delete the picture from your Downloads.
2. Type the name and choose a category (or **+ New category...**).
3. Choose **By quantity** (many identical pieces) or **One of a kind** (one piece with its own number, like a scooter's chassis number).
4. Fill in the details. If you need a detail that is not there, press **+ Add a field**. Choose if it is for all items in this category or only this item. What you already typed stays.
5. Press **Save item**.

## Finding things

- **Items & stock**: search by name, number or any detail. Use **More filters** for dates bought or sold, stock on a date, or details like "drawer count is 4".
- **History**: every purchase, sale, return and correction. Tick the type and choose dates. For example tick Purchases and choose 15 March to see what you bought that day.
- Open any item to see its photos, details, purchases, sales and every stock movement.

## Reports

Choose a month or your own dates. You see what you bought and spent, what you sold, refunds, net sales, the cost of what you sold, gross profit and stock on hand. Click any box to see the records behind it. **Export** saves a spreadsheet file.

## Mistakes

Nothing is ever deleted. To fix a mistake, open the sale or purchase in History and press:
- **Return items**: a customer brings some items back. The refund is worked out for you.
- **Reverse**: cancel the whole record. Write the reason. Both the record and its reversal stay in history.
- On an item page, **Correct stock** when your count is different (broken, lost, found). Write the reason.

Items, categories and fields you no longer use can be **Archived**. They disappear from daily lists but their history stays and can still be searched.

## Backups (please read)

Your records are saved on this computer. A computer can break, so keep a second copy:

1. Plug in a USB drive or second disk.
2. **Settings, Backups, Choose drive**, pick the drive.
3. The app makes a backup by itself every hour while open and checks it. The left side shows when the last backup finished.

If the drive is not plugged in, a yellow message tells you. Plug it in and the app tries again by itself. You can also press **Back up now**.

Keep the USB drive somewhere safe. A second drive kept at home is even better: plug it in once a week and press Back up now.

## New or repaired computer

1. Install ShopPOS (the installer from your developer).
2. Open it and choose **Restore from backup**.
3. Plug in your backup drive, press **Choose backup drive**, pick the newest backup, press **Restore**.

All items, photos, receipts, settings and your logo come back.

## Updates

You do not need to do anything. When your developer sends an update, close the app and open it again. It installs the update by itself and keeps all your records.

## Your shop look

**Settings**: shop name, logo, address, phone, money sign, date style, receipt text, colours and text size. In **Categories & fields**, press **Change** on a field to show it in lists or print it on receipts.
